### SUS scoring

Calculate a SUS score only when all ten items have valid responses. Code responses 1–5. For odd-numbered items
(1, 3, 5, 7, 9), contribution = response − 1. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10), contribution =
5 − response. Sum the ten contributions and multiply by 2.5. The resulting score ranges from 0 to 100; it is
not a percentage, probability, or individual performance grade. Do not impute missing responses. Report the
number of complete questionnaires and describe this formative sample; do not use a conventional benchmark as
proof of usability or generalize a small-sample score to a wider population.

## Open-ended questions

1. Which interaction, if any, was confusing or harder than you expected?
2. Was any status, error, or instruction unclear? What did you expect it to say or do?
3. What did you understand the match score to mean? What information would help you interpret it?
4. Was any result or explanation easy to over-interpret? Please describe without naming real people or employers.
5. What, if anything, would you change about the workflow, wording, comparison, or privacy information?
6. Is there anything else about your experience you would like the research team to know?

Do not ask participants to assess algorithmic fairness or job-performance validity from this short interaction.
If they volunteer such views, record them as perceptions, not evidence that the system is fair or valid.
